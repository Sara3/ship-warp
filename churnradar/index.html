How the score works

Each account gets risk points from three facts. Add them up. 0-1 points = Green, 2-3 = Amber, 4-6 = Red. Plain rule, no AI in the score.

Fact2 points1 point0 points
Logins in the last 30 days0-45-1415 or more
Open support tickets5 or more2-40-1
Days to renewal0-30 (or overdue)31-9091 or more

The "AI read" column is advice from the account notes only. It never changes the Red / Amber / Green score.

boleto/Pix Overdue payments are flagged separately and do not change the score. Any account 1 or more days overdue is pinned near the top of the at-risk list.

Escalations

A cancellation warning opens one automatically. Use "Escalate" on any at-risk account to open one by hand.
